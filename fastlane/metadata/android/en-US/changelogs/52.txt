Your phone now keeps its own copy of your index, so browsing and syncing ask Opensolr for nothing.

- Tagging is instant: saved on the phone, carried up on its own, 50 photos per call, no pictures sent.
- People first in the tagging form, with an Add or Replace switch for names and for tags.
- Long press starts selecting; it ends when the last tick goes. A tick on a heading takes the whole group.
- Photos grouped by year, then month, then day, with every group and its count there from the start.
- Filters fold away, are remembered, and show how many of their own filters are on. Camera make removed.
- Map: a tap opens the photos of a place, the sheet drags to full screen and names the place. Night map dimmed, not inverted.
- Pull down to reload and sync.
- Fixed: a photo with no date of its own no longer jumps to today when tagged; text already read out of photos is kept when a later pass cannot read it.
