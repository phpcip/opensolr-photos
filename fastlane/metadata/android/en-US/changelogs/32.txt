Photos are now searched by the text printed in them: a petrol receipt is found by the station, the total or its number, a shelf label by its product code, a screenshot by what it says. Reading the text happens on the Opensolr side, on servers that do nothing else, and it costs the same as before — a tenth of a request per photo. Your index is reset once and re-synced so the text of every photo you already have is read too; the tags and the words you wrote yourself are kept.
