A strictest duplicates stop that finds true copies: the phone now works out the fingerprint of each photo file, so only files that are byte for byte the same group together. Groups fold away with a tap and stay folded. Photos you have tagged carry a small tag in their corner, and a Tagged / Not tagged filter shows one or the other. The Folder filter is gone: Android names folders by their whole path, so the list was unusable. Deleting photos no longer throws the grid somewhere random, and tagging many photos shows its progress while it runs.
