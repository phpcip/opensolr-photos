Pick photos by dragging: press and hold one, keep your finger down, and everything between it and the photo under your finger is ticked as you go - drag back to untick, and the grid scrolls itself near the edges so a run can pass what is on screen. The bar down the right edge is now a map of how tall the grid really is, not of how many rows it has, so a folded group no longer takes as much of it as a single photo and your finger can stop on one. Searching for a place shows the commune under the name, so two villages with the same name in the same county are no longer the same line twice.
