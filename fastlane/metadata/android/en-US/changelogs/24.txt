Answers from your index are now really reused: they were being fetched and then thrown away whenever you kept moving, so the duplicates slider asked again for everything it had already asked for. The photos grid also returns to where you left it, one place per search, so coming back from an album, from the similar photos or from your account lands where you were instead of at the top. When something goes wrong you get a plain sentence rather than a Java message: no connection, a slow index, a failed secure connection.
