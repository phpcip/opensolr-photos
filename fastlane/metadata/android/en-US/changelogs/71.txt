Grouping by folder now shows your folders as the tree they are - Pictures, then 2019, then 11 - instead of a flat list of last names, which on a library kept by year and month read as a column of numbers. A folder holding nothing but one other folder is written on one line, and photos lying directly in a folder that also holds folders get a line of their own. The similar photos slider glides now, and leaves a stop only once your finger is most of the way to the next one.
