Photos now carry the names of the people in them. If something has already recognised the faces on your photos - Google Photos, Lightroom, digiKam - those names travel with the file, and Opensolr Photos now reads them and makes them searchable: type a name and you get that person's photos. Names with diacritics are found either way, written properly or plainly.

This version resets your index so the names can be written into it. Your tags are kept.

Also fixed: the strictest duplicates level, "Same file (exact copy)", never had any data to work with - the file fingerprint the app computes was being dropped before it reached the index. It works now.
